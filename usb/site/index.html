It works.

This page is being served from a USB stick on your own hardware, behind your own router. No hosting company, no monthly bill, no platform that can take it down.

What is this?

Make it yours

  1. Open the admin panel (the address was printed when the server started).
  2. Sign in and change the generated password.
  3. Edit index.html right in the browser, or upload your own files.
  4. Follow docs/03-going-online.md to put it on the public internet.

Everything in the site/ folder is published exactly as it sits on disk. Drop in an existing static site and it will just work.
